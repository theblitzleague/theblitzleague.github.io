# Official Rules of The Blitz League

Nov 6, 2026 · @Gavin

These rules govern every Franchise, Owner and game of The Blitz League, starting with the founding 2027 Season. Rules are organized by **Rule**, **Section** and **Article**, and are cited as Rule-Section-Article (for example, 6-3-1). **Approved Rulings (A.R.)** show how a rule is applied, and **Penalty** lines set the consequence for breaking it.

## Rule 1. Franchise Identity and Naming

### Section 1. Selection and Approval

**Article 1. Founding owner's choice.** Each founding owner may select an available league-created franchise or propose an original franchise identity. An original identity must pair a real city or state with a team nickname. Owners choose the city or state their franchise represents, usually where they live. Geographic designations and nicknames must be distinct from those of other Blitz League franchises unless the Commissioner's Office grants an exception.

**Article 2. Naming standard.** A franchise name must be suitable for the league schedule, public broadcasts and official records. Names drawn from regional history, folklore, local character, or traditional football imagery are welcome. The league will decline names built around profanity, slurs, sexual references, personal attacks, inside jokes, or temporary memes. A franchise may not copy an existing professional team's identity or imply an official affiliation with it.

**Article 3. Submission and competing choices.** All founding owners will receive the same submission deadline. Each owner must submit a first choice and a backup. For an original identity, the submission must include the full franchise name, proposed abbreviation, and a brief explanation. Cities and states are first come, first served: the first owner to submit a market holds it once their identity is approved, and a later owner chooses another. Two franchises may share a market only with the Commissioner's Office's approval, as two NFL teams share Los Angeles.

**Article 4. Commissioner approval.** The Commissioner's Office has final authority to approve a selection, request revisions, or deny it. If a proposal is denied, the office will explain the reason and allow the owner to submit another choice. Owners must receive written approval before publicly announcing a franchise identity. The approved name and spelling will be used in league records and on awards.

### Section 2. Continuity

**Article 1. Franchise continuity.** The approved identity belongs to the franchise and carries forward through a change in ownership. Once approved, a franchise's name is final, so that the League's history, statistics and honors stay consistent. A franchise is renamed or relocated only if the Commissioner's Office agrees in writing. Championships, banners, and historical records retain the franchise name under which the franchise earned them.

> **A.R. 1.1** The Chicago franchise wins the 2027 championship, then is renamed with approval in 2029. **Ruling:** The 2027 title stays in the record book under the Chicago name.

## Rule 2. Definitions

### Section 1. League Terms

**Article 1. League.** The Blitz League, founded in 2026.

**Article 2. Commissioner's Office.** The Commissioner of The Blitz League, acting in that role.

**Article 3. Owner.** The person who holds a Franchise and serves as its General Manager. The two titles refer to the same person.

**Article 4. Franchise.** One of the League's 12 member teams, including its approved identity, roster, draft picks and history.

**Article 5. Blitz Hub.** The League's own app, served from the League Website, where the League runs its drafts, transactions, lineups and scoring.

**Article 6. League Website.** theblitzleague.github.io, the League's official site of record.

**Article 7. League Chat.** The League's official group chat.

### Section 2. Calendar and Voting Terms

**Article 1. Season.** The period from the rookie draft through the championship game.

**Article 2. Offseason.** The period from the end of the championship game to the start of the rookie draft.

**Article 3. Final.** A week is final at 5 AM Central on the Tuesday after its last game, when Blitz Hub records that week's official scores.

**Article 4. In writing.** A message in the League Chat, a text or an email that identifies who sent it.

**Article 5. Supermajority.** At least 8 of the 12 Owners.

## Rule 3. League Structure

### Section 1. Franchises and Divisions

**Article 1. Membership.** The League has 12 Franchises, each held by one Owner. The number of Franchises changes only under Rule 13.

**Article 2. Divisions.** The Franchises play in three divisions of four:

| --- | --- |
| Cutthroat Capital | East |
| Murderers Row | Central |
| Black Market | West |

**Article 3. Division assignment.** A Franchise plays in the division for its region. Its division changes only if it relocates with approval under 1-2-1.

### Section 2. Format and Blitz Hub

**Article 1. Dynasty format.** Rosters, draft picks and records carry over from Season to Season. No roster is reset.

**Article 2. Official results.** All drafts, transactions, lineups and scoring take place on Blitz Hub. Final scores on Blitz Hub are the official results.

**Article 3. Site of record.** The League Website is the official record of Franchise identities, standings, honors and history. It is also where every draft pick, transaction and lineup is made, through Blitz Hub (2-1-5).

**Article 4. Conflicts.** When these rules and Blitz Hub's settings disagree, these rules govern. The Commissioner's Office corrects Blitz Hub to match.

## Rule 4. Commissioner's Office

### Section 1. Authority

**Article 1. Administration.** The Commissioner's Office runs the League. It holds League funds, controls Blitz Hub's settings, keeps the official records and the League Website, and interprets these rules.

**Article 2. Situations not covered.** The Commissioner's Office rules on any situation these rules do not cover, in keeping with their intent.

**Article 3. Rulings.** Rulings are issued to all Owners in writing. A ruling that interprets a rule is recorded and guides later rulings on the same question.

**Article 4. Co-Commissioners.** The Commissioner appoints two Co-Commissioners from among the Owners, and may replace either at any time. A Co-Commissioner acts for the Commissioner's Office when the Commissioner asks, and steps aside from any matter involving their own Franchise.

### Section 2. Disputes and Appeals

**Article 1. Filing.** An Owner must raise a dispute with the Commissioner's Office within 24 hours of the action in question.

**Article 2. Ruling.** The Commissioner and the two Co-Commissioners review the dispute together and rule within 72 hours. A ruling needs 2 of the 3 votes. A member whose own Franchise is involved does not vote; if the two who remain disagree, the uninvolved Owners vote.

**Article 3. Appeal.** Within 24 hours of that ruling, an Owner may appeal it to a vote of the Owners, with the written support of a second Owner. A ruling may be appealed only once, and the vote's result is final. The ruling stands unless a Supermajority votes to overturn it.

**Article 4. Not subject to a vote.** The following may not be appealed or put to a vote: the Commissioner's office and ownership of the League (4-4-2); Franchise names and identities once approved (Rule 1); draft dates, the schedule, and Blitz Hub settings these rules already fix; and dues and prizes for a Season already paid for (6-4-5).

### Section 3. Conflicts of Interest

**Article 1. Recusal.** The Commissioner is also an Owner, and steps aside from any matter involving their own Franchise. The two Co-Commissioners decide it instead. If they disagree, or if either is involved, the uninvolved Owners vote.

### Section 4. Succession

**Article 1. Resignation.** The Commissioner may resign by notice in writing. The Owners then elect a new Commissioner by majority vote.

**Article 2. Removal.** The Commissioner founded the League and owns it, and may not be removed from the office by any vote.

## Rule 5. Owners and General Managers

### Section 1. Appointment

**Article 1. Admission.** An Owner joins the League by invitation of the Commissioner's Office. They take office once they have paid their dues. By joining, every Owner agrees to these rules.

**Article 2. Term.** Ownership is a year-round appointment. It continues from Season to Season until the Owner leaves or is removed under Rule 11.

**Article 3. One Franchise.** No person may hold or control more than one Franchise, directly or through another person.

### Section 2. Duties

**Article 1. Football operations.** The Owner directs the Franchise's football operations on Blitz Hub. That covers draft picks, trades and setting a competitive lineup every week.

**Article 2. Year-round duties.** In the Offseason, the Owner's duties include the rookie draft, League communications and every deadline in Appendix A.

**Article 3. Contact.** Every Owner keeps a working contact method on file with the Commissioner's Office and answers League business within 72 hours.

### Section 3. Voting

**Article 1. One vote.** Each Franchise has one vote on League matters, cast in writing.

**Article 2. Voting period.** A vote stays open for at least 48 hours, unless every Owner has voted sooner.

## Rule 6. Dues and Prize Money

### Section 1. Dues

**Article 1. Amount.** Annual dues are $100 per Owner per Season, payable in advance. Dues are paid, and prizes paid out, by Venmo, sent as Friends & Family with the note "Blitz League dues" and the Season.

**Article 2. Due date.** Founding-Season dues are due by March 13, 2027, one week before the startup draft. An Owner whose founding dues are still unpaid on March 18, 2027 loses their seat before the startup draft: a replacement Owner takes the Franchise and drafts it, or, if none is ready, the Commissioner's Office drafts it and holds it for a successor under Rule 11, Section 3. Dues for every later Season are due before that Season's rookie draft.

**Article 3. Unpaid dues.** After the founding Season, an Owner with unpaid dues may not make draft picks, trades or waiver claims until the dues are paid.

**Penalty:** Dues still unpaid 14 days after the due date are grounds for removal under 11-2-2.

### Section 2. Prize Fund

**Article 1. The fund.** All dues go into the Prize Fund, which the Commissioner's Office holds for the Owners. With 12 Franchises the Prize Fund is $1,200, and all of it is paid out.

**Article 2. Payout schedule.** The current schedule is also published on the League Website.

### Section 3. Determining Winners

**Article 1. Weekly high score.** Goes to the Franchise with the highest score in that regular-season week.

**Article 2. Division winner.** The Franchise with the best regular-season record in its division. Ties are broken by points for.

**Article 3. Points leader.** The Franchise with the most regular-season points for. Playoff points do not count.

**Article 4. General Manager of the Year.** Honor only, with no prize money: chosen by a vote of the Owners at the end of the Season. Third place goes to the winner of the third-place game between the two divisional-round losers.

**Article 5. Ties.** Franchises that tie for a prize split it equally.

> **A.R. 6.1** In Week 6, two Franchises tie for the high score with 168.40 points. **Ruling:** Each receives $7.50.

> **A.R. 6.2** A Franchise leads the League in points through Week 14, then scores the most points again in the playoffs. **Ruling:** The points-leader prize is based on Weeks 1-14 only. Playoff points never count toward it.

### Section 4. Payment and Records

**Article 1. Weekly prizes.** Paid within 7 days of the week going final.

**Article 2. Season prizes.** Paid within 14 days of the championship game.

**Article 3. Ledger.** Every payment in and out is recorded in the League's ledger.

**Article 4. Honors without prize money.** General Manager of the Year, Offensive Player of the Year, Defensive Player of the Year, Rookie of the Year, Pickup of the Year and Trade of the Year carry a place in the record book, but no prize money.

**Article 5. Changes.** Dues and the payout schedule change only in the Offseason under Rule 13, and never for a Season that has already been paid for.

## Rule 7. Season, Scoring and Lineups

### Section 1. Schedule

**Article 1. Regular season.** Weeks 1 through 14 of the NFL season. Each Franchise plays one head-to-head game per week.

**Article 2. Playoffs.** Six Franchises qualify, seeded as in the NFL. The three division winners (6-3-2) are seeds 1 to 3, ranked by regular-season record. The three Franchises with the best records among the rest are the wild cards, seeds 4 to 6. Ties are broken by points for. Seeds 1 and 2 get a first-round bye. The playoffs begin in Week 15 with the wild card round: seed 3 against seed 6 and seed 4 against seed 5. Week 16 is the divisional round: the top seed plays the lowest seed left and the other seeds meet. In Week 17 the divisional winners play in the Blitz Bowl for the championship, and the losers play the third-place game. A tied playoff game goes to the higher seed. The winner of the championship game is the League Champion.

### Section 2. Starting Lineup

**Article 1. Positions.** Each Franchise starts 14 players every week. There is no team defense slot.

| Superflex | 1 | QB, RB, WR, TE |
| Kicker | 1 | K |
| Defensive line | 1 | DL |

### Section 3. Scoring

**Article 1. Scoring settings.** Points are scored under the League's scoring settings on Blitz Hub, listed in full in Appendix B. They include 1 point per reception, 4 per passing touchdown, minus 2 per interception thrown, 1 per tackle (solo or assisted) and 2 per sack by a defensive player.

**Article 2. Changes.** A scoring change requires a vote under Rule 13 and takes effect the following Season, unless all 12 Owners vote for it to take effect sooner. A scoring change never alters a game already played.

**Article 3. Stat corrections.** Stat corrections Blitz Hub applies before the week goes final are part of the official score. Corrections after that do not change any result or prize.

### Section 4. Lineups

**Article 1. Setting lineups.** Each Owner sets their lineup on Blitz Hub.

**Article 2. Lock.** Each player locks at his game's kickoff. No lineup is changed after the fact for any reason. A starter whose game has kicked off stays in that week's lineup, and his points stand, whatever happens to him afterward.

**Article 3. Competitive lineups.** Every Owner starts a full lineup every week, including the playoffs. Every slot must hold a player who is active or expected to play.

**Penalty:** For knowingly starting an injured, inactive, suspended or bye-week player when a healthy player is available on the roster: a warning for a first offense. A repeat offense is treated as tanking under 10-1-3.

> **A.R. 7.1** An Owner starts a wide receiver who was ruled out on Friday and has a healthy receiver on the bench. **Ruling:** A violation of 7-4-3. The result of the game stands.

## Rule 8. Rosters, Drafts and Transactions

### Section 1. Roster Limits

**Article 1. Roster.** Each Franchise carries 14 starters, 10 bench players, up to 5 taxi squad players and up to 4 injured reserve players.

**Article 2. Taxi squad.** Only rookies may be placed on the taxi squad. A player may stay there while he is within the taxi experience limit of 2 years. The taxi squad opens once the startup draft is complete, and is first filled from the founding Season's rookie draft. Taxi moves follow the taxi deadline in Week 4.

**Article 3. Injured reserve.** Only a player Blitz Hub lists as on injured reserve or out may occupy an IR slot. When his status clears, he must be activated or released before the Owner's next transaction.

**Article 4. Quarterback limit.** A Franchise may carry no more than 2 quarterbacks. Quarterbacks on the taxi squad or injured reserve do not count toward the limit. Blitz Hub enforces it: no Franchise may draft past it, and a Franchise over it cannot change its lineup until it is back within it.

### Section 2. Drafts

**Article 1. Startup draft.** The founding rosters are built in one startup draft of 24 rounds in linear order, one round for each roster spot. Only players already in the NFL are eligible; that year's rookies enter through the rookie draft. It is drafted live on Blitz Hub on March 20 and 21, 2027, one week after NFL free agency opens, at start times the Commissioner's Office announces in writing. The Commissioner's Office announces the draft order in writing.

**Article 2. Rookie draft.** Every Offseason the League holds a rookie draft of 4 rounds.

**Article 3. Rookie draft order.** Franchises that missed the playoffs pick first, worst regular-season record first. Playoff Franchises follow in reverse order of finish. The League Champion picks last in every round.

**Article 4. Pick clock.** Each Owner picks within the clock set by the Commissioner's Office. An Owner who misses a pick receives Blitz Hub's automatic pick, taken from their draft queue if they set one. An Owner who can't attend may instead ask the Commissioner's Office to make their picks from a list they provide; no other Owner may pick for them. Picks made by the clock or by the Commissioner's Office stand, and no draft is redone.

### Section 3. Trades

**Article 1. Eligible assets.** Owners may trade players and draft picks, including picks up to three Seasons ahead.

**Article 2. Review period.** Every accepted trade sits in a two-day review period on Blitz Hub. When review ends, the trade is processed in the next weekly moves batch, at 3 AM Central on Wednesday, once the week's scores are official and before any game of the new week.

**Article 3. Trade deadline.** No trade may be accepted after the Week 11 trade deadline, which falls when the last NFL game of Week 11 ends. A trade accepted before then still processes after its review period, even if that period ends after the deadline. Trading reopens when the championship game is final.

**Article 4. Reversal.** The Commissioner's Office reverses a trade only for collusion or a rules violation, never because one side got the better of the deal. A trade involving the Commissioner's own Franchise is handled under 4-3-1.

**Article 5. Trading future picks.** Before trading away a draft pick in a future Season, an Owner must prepay half of that Season's dues ($50). The prepayment is credited toward that Season's dues and is not refundable. If the Owner leaves or is removed before that Season, the prepayment stays with the League and is credited toward their successor's dues for that Season.

> **A.R. 8.1** In 2027, an Owner offers their 2029 first-round pick in a trade. **Ruling:** The trade cannot be accepted until that Owner has prepaid $50 toward their 2029 dues. They owe the other $50 before the 2029 rookie draft, and they do not need to prepay anything for 2028, since they are not trading a 2028 pick.

> **A.R. 8.2** Two Owners accept a trade during the Monday night game of Week 11, and its two-day review ends on Wednesday evening of Week 12. **Ruling:** The trade is valid, because it was accepted before the last game of Week 11 ended. Blitz Hub processes it in the next weekly moves batch, at 3 AM Central the following Wednesday.

### Section 4. Waivers and Free Agents

**Article 1. Waiver period.** A dropped player is on waivers for at least two days. Waivers clear in the weekly moves batch, at 3 AM Central on Wednesday: a player whose two days are up by then goes to the winning claim, or becomes a free agent if nobody claimed him. A player who isn't on waivers is a free agent and may be added at any time.

**Article 2. Claims.** Waiver claims are decided by blind bidding from a $100 budget each Season, which does not carry over. The minimum bid is $1. The highest bid wins and is charged to the winner's budget; a tied bid goes to the Franchise with the worse record (fewer wins, then fewer points for), and if still tied, to the claim placed first.

**Article 3. Offseason.** No free agents may be added in the Offseason.

## Rule 9. Wagers

### Section 1. Permitted Wagers

**Article 1. Wagers.** Owners may wager with each other on any game, in the regular season or the playoffs. Wagers are voluntary and are kept separate from the Prize Fund. The Commissioner's Office never holds wager money.

**Article 2. Never against your own Franchise.** An Owner may never wager on their own Franchise to lose, since that rewards losing on purpose. Doing so is tanking under 10-1-3.

### Section 2. Private Wagers

**Article 1. Private wagers.** Every wager is a private matter between the Owners who make it. The League does not track, settle or enforce it.

> **A.R. 9.1** Two Owners agree by text to a $25 wager on another Franchise's game, and the loser never pays. **Ruling:** A private matter between the two Owners. The League does not enforce it.

### Section 3. Responsibility

**Article 1. No sportsbook.** The League does not run a sportsbook and is not a party to any wager. Each Owner is responsible for making sure their wagers are lawful where they live.

## Rule 10. Integrity of Competition

### Section 1. Prohibited Conduct

**Article 1. Good faith.** Every Owner tries to win every game and deals with every other Owner in good faith.

**Article 2. Collusion.** Collusion is any arrangement between Owners that helps one Franchise at the League's expense. It includes agreeing to trade players back later, making a trade on another Owner's behalf, and making a trade to help another Owner win in exchange for a share of their prize money.

**Penalty:** The trade is reversed, and every Owner involved receives a written warning.

**Article 3. Tanking.** Rebuilding is permitted, but deliberately losing is not. Setting a lineup to lose, or leaving a lineup unset in order to lose, is tanking.

**Penalty:** First offense: a written warning. Second offense: the Owner's first-round pick in the next rookie draft moves back one spot. Third and later offenses: that pick moves to the last pick of the first round.

**Article 4. Trash talk.** Trash talk of any kind is welcome and is part of the League. No rule limits it, and it carries no penalty.

> **A.R. 10.1** An Owner eliminated from playoff contention benches healthy starters in Week 13 so a rival loses the division. **Ruling:** Tanking under 10-1-3, and possible collusion under 10-1-2 if the Owners arranged it.

### Section 2. Sanctions

**Article 1. Available sanctions.** For any violation of these rules, the Commissioner's Office may issue a written warning or move or take away draft picks. No Owner loses prize money or is removed as a sanction.

**Article 2. Appeal.** Every sanction may be appealed under 4-2-3.

## Rule 11. Change of Ownership

### Section 1. Voluntary Departure

**Article 1. Notice.** An Owner who wants to leave gives the Commissioner's Office notice in writing during the Offseason. Leaving during the Season isn't permitted, except in an emergency the Commissioner's Office accepts in writing.

**Article 2. No refunds.** Dues are not refunded to an Owner who leaves or is removed. That includes dues prepaid under 8-3-5.

**Article 3. Franchise remains.** The Franchise stays with the League in full: its identity, roster, draft picks and history. Its identity carries forward under 1-2-1. The departing Owner keeps their personal career record and honors.

**Article 4. No trades after notice.** An Owner may not trade once they have given notice. The Commissioner's Office reviews every trade the Owner made in the 30 days before notice, and may reverse any trade that strips the Franchise for another Owner's benefit.

### Section 2. Abandonment and Removal

**Article 1. Abandonment.** An Owner is considered to have left the League if they set no lineup for two straight weeks, or don't respond to the Commissioner's Office for 14 days. The Commissioner's Office sets that Franchise's lineups until a successor takes over.

**Article 2. Removal.** The Owners may remove an Owner other than the Commissioner by Supermajority vote only for abandonment under 11-2-1 or unpaid dues under 6-1-3. The Owner in question does not vote. Removal takes effect immediately, and 11-1-2 through 11-1-4 apply.

### Section 3. Successor Owners

**Article 1. Selection.** The Commissioner's Office recruits a successor, and the Owners approve them by majority vote.

**Article 2. Terms.** The successor pays the current Season's dues and takes over the Franchise as it stands. They may ask for a new identity under 1-2-1.

> **A.R. 11.1** An Owner gives notice in February, then offers their best player for two late-round picks. **Ruling:** The trade is not allowed under 11-1-4.

## Rule 12. League Records

### Section 1. Record Book

**Article 1. Official record.** The Commissioner's Office keeps the League record book on the League Website. It covers every Season's standings, champions, division winners, prize winners and award winners.

**Article 2. Credit for titles.** A championship or division title is credited to the Franchise, under the name it held at the time, and to the Owner who won it. An Owner keeps their titles if they later hold a different Franchise.

### Section 2. Annual Honors

**Article 1. Awards.** At the end of each Season the League names a General Manager of the Year, by vote of the Owners. It also names an Offensive Player of the Year, a Defensive Player of the Year, a Rookie of the Year, a Pickup of the Year and a Trade of the Year.

## Rule 13. Rule Changes

### Section 1. Proposals and Adoption

**Article 1. Proposals.** Any Owner may propose a rule change to the Commissioner's Office in writing. Every proposal received in the Offseason goes to a vote of the Owners before the rookie draft.

**Article 2. Adoption.** A rule change passes with a Supermajority vote. It takes effect at the start of the next Season unless the change itself says otherwise. 4-4-2 (the Commissioner's office) and 4-2-4 (matters not subject to a vote) may be changed only with the Commissioner's agreement in writing, and no vote may change this sentence.

**Article 3. In-season changes.** During the Season, a rule may change only to fix an error or close a loophole that threatens fair competition. Such a change needs a Supermajority vote and does not change the result of any game already played. A scoring change may also take effect during the Season, but only by a unanimous vote of all 12 Owners (7-3-2).

### Section 2. Adoption of These Rules

**Article 1. Effective date.** These rules take effect when the Commissioner's Office publishes them on the League Website.

**Article 2. Later Owners.** Every Owner, founding or admitted later, is bound by these rules by joining the League under 5-1-1.

**Article 3. Official text.** The Commissioner's Office keeps the current rules on the League Website, along with a dated record of every change.
